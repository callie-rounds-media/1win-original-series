What your competitors are doing

Your competitors pay for attention, but none of them has built a show yet.

Stake

Sponsors Everton and the UFC, put its name on an F1 team, and its founders back the streaming platform Kick. It has also paid for 220 sponsored YouTube videos across 25 channels.

Owns a platform, not a show. Every video is a one-off ad read inside someone else's content.

1xBet

Signed Conor McGregor and has sponsored FC Barcelona since 2019. Co-funded one esports documentary series, OG's "Origins", in 2024.

One co-funded documentary, made for an esports team, not for 1xBet.

How other restricted brands did it

The restricted brands that won did it under a second name.

OnlyFans · OFTV
Netflix

OnlyFans launched OFTV in 2021 as a free app with no adult content, because the main site could not get into the app stores. Its reality show "House of Sims" later became the first show Netflix ever licensed from OnlyFans.

1.1bn hrs

Kick is backed by Stake's founders and carries a clean name of its own, which ran on the F1 car alongside Stake's. It paid xQc $70 million guaranteed and passed 1.1 billion hours watched in the second quarter of 2025.

Red Bull Media House
1,000+

Red Bull turned its content into a business with over 1,000 distribution partners, including free TV channels on Samsung, Pluto, Roku, LG and JioTV in India. Every project has to earn its money back through ads, licensing or co-production.

What did not work

FanDuel turned a racing channel into FanDuel TV in 2022 and announced it was shutting it down in March 2026. Penn bought Barstool for $551 million and sold it back for $1. Both put the betting brand front and centre and paid for a full TV operation before they had a hit.

What changed in 2026

The rules now follow the brand name, not what is shown on screen.

India · from 1 May 2026
Promoting real-money gaming directly or indirectly is now a crime

This covers offshore operators and the influencers who promote them, and commentators read it as covering content framed as entertainment.

Brazil · from 25 Sept 2026
All betting advertising and sponsorship is banned

The decree covers any content that promotes or makes it easier to reach betting, and its validity was extended on 10 November.

YouTube · since March 2025
Logos, links and spoken mentions count

YouTube removes content that points viewers to gambling sites Google has not certified, even when no betting is shown.

This is exactly why OnlyFans built OFTV and why Stake's founders built Kick.

A show with its own name and no gambling in it can be watched, recommended and licensed anywhere. 1win funds it and is credited on it in the markets where that is allowed, and a clean version goes everywhere else. We would build the show this way from day one and check the credit plan with your compliance team before anything is filmed.

Where it could be watched

Launch where the audience already is, then license it out.

Launch

YouTube

Free to watch, mostly on TV screens, and it can be fully recommended and monetised as long as there is no gambling in the show. Short cutdowns run on Instagram and YouTube Shorts.

Second window

Free streaming apps

Tubi, The Roku Channel and Pluto license finished seasons and usually split ad revenue around 50/50. House of Sims is on Tubi today.

Regional

Streamers in your markets

JioHotstar's creator section made 30 creator-led shows with nearly 300 Indian creators in nine months, and Globo just licensed 18 of its originals to Netflix.

The upside

Netflix or Prime Video

This is the House of Sims and Sidemen route. It is earned with audience numbers rather than promised, which is why the first season should be built to be licensable.

Later, not first: a 1win app or a 24/7 channel. A free TV channel needs around 300 hours of content, and OnlyFans and Red Bull both had large libraries before going that way. Not at all: TikTok, which does not allow gambling sponsors of any kind.

Three ways to do it

We think the third option is the strongest.

Option one

A 1win TV channel or app

The most direct version of OFTV, with 1win's name on everything.

  • The most brand credit per viewer
  • The fewest viewers, because the name is what the rules restrict
  • Needs a big library before it is worth launching
Option two

Sponsored videos inside other creators' content

What Stake does with its 220 YouTube integrations.

  • Quick and already proven
  • Nothing is left once the campaign ends
  • It is the same thing your competitors already buy
Option three · our pick

An original series with its own name, funded by 1win

The OFTV and Kick approach at the size of one show, starting with a pilot.

  • Can be watched, recommended and licensed everywhere
  • 1win is credited wherever that is allowed
  • Builds a library 1win's fighters and esports team can join in later seasons
